AI Output Quality &
Hallucination Firewall

An evidence-backed verification layer that sits between an LLM and the end user, checks generated claims against trusted sources, calculates measurable support signals, and applies a PASS, WARN, or REJECT policy before the answer is trusted.

PRIMARY FOCUSAI Reliability & Hallucination Mitigation
PLATFORMAmazon Bedrock + Python
DESIGN PATTERNGenerate → Retrieve → Verify → Decide
ARCHITECTURE FLOW

How the firewall evaluates AI output

REQUESTUser Question

A user submits a question or candidate answer for verification.

GENERATEAmazon Bedrock

A foundation model generates the candidate response.

RETRIEVETrusted Evidence

Bedrock Knowledge Bases retrieves authoritative source material.

VERIFYClaim Analysis

Claims are extracted and measured against retrieved evidence.

ENFORCEFirewall Decision

The quality policy returns PASS, WARN, or REJECT.

Technology Stack

Amazon BedrockKnowledge BasesPythonStreamlitRAGAI GuardrailsAWS IAMCloudWatch

Decision Policy

PASSScore ≥ 80
WARNScore 50–79
REJECTScore < 50

Security & Quality Controls

●Generation and verification are separated so model confidence is not treated as evidence.
●Claims are checked against an explicitly controlled authoritative evidence boundary.
●Unsupported claims are surfaced instead of silently passed to the user.
●Evidence provenance and retrieval relevance can be displayed in the dashboard.
●Architecture is designed for future audit logging, CloudWatch metrics, and measurable evaluation.
